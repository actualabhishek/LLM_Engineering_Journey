# Velvet Vista Hotel

Velvet Vista is a 4-star hotel in Udaipur, Rajasthan, overlooking the lake. It has 55 rooms across five categories, a spa, a rooftop pool, a gym, two restaurants and banquet space for weddings and conferences.

## Room types

- **Standard Room** - Cosy room with a queen bed, city view and free Wi-Fi. Good for solo travellers and couples.
- **Deluxe Room** - Spacious room with a king bed, a lounge chair and a garden view.
- **Executive Suite** - Separate living area, work desk and access to the executive lounge.
- **Family Suite** - Two-room suite with a bunk nook, ideal for families with children.
- **Presidential Suite** - Top-floor suite with a private terrace, lake view and butler service.

Live prices and availability always come from the booking system, never quote a price from memory.

## Amenities

- Rooftop infinity pool, open 6 AM - 9 PM.
- Full-service spa: massages, facials and steam room, open 9 AM - 8 PM (book at least 2 hours ahead).
- 24-hour gym with cardio and weight equipment.
- Free Wi-Fi throughout the property.
- Free parking for guests.
- Airport shuttle on request (charged separately, arranged at the front desk).

## Dining

- **Lake View Restaurant**: multi-cuisine, breakfast 7-10:30 AM, lunch 12:30-3 PM, dinner 7-11 PM.
- **Spice Route**: rooftop restaurant specialising in North Indian and Rajasthani cuisine, open 6 PM - midnight.
- **Cafe Mez**: all-day coffee, snacks and light meals in the lobby.
- Room service is available 24 hours.

## Check-in / check-out

- Standard check-in: 2 PM. Standard check-out: 11 AM.
- Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability; ask the front desk on arrival.
- Guests can complete check-in ahead of arrival through this assistant by confirming their arrival time and any special requests.

## Cancellation policy

Bookings can be cancelled any time with no cancellation fee, since payment is always made at the hotel, not online.

## Pet and smoking policy

- Pets are not allowed, except registered service animals.
- All rooms and indoor areas are non-smoking. A designated smoking area is available near the garden.

## Loyalty program

Three tiers: Silver, Gold and Platinum. Guests earn points automatically on every stay and can redeem points for a future stay through this assistant.

- **Silver** (starting tier): a welcome amenity on arrival.
- **Gold**: Silver benefits, plus a complimentary room upgrade when available and late check-out until 1 PM on request.
- **Platinum**: Gold benefits, plus a welcome drink at check-in and priority spa booking.

Tier is set when a guest enrolls and doesn't change automatically as points accumulate; exact points balances and redemptions always come from the loyalty system, never quoted from memory.

## Local tips

- The City Palace and lake boat rides are a short walk from the hotel.
- Sunset at the rooftop restaurant is a popular time to visit Spice Route; arrive by 6:30 PM for the best tables.
- The old city markets for handicrafts and textiles are about 10 minutes away by auto-rickshaw.
